# FLIM Studio 0.10.3

## Fixed: phasor plot looked like a stray LabVIEW window

Root cause: `phasor_plot()` was the only plot left rendering LabVIEW's grey
panel / near-black text palette (`#b9b9b9` background, `#111111` text),
while every other plot in the app (`line_plot`-based Decay & fit,
Histogram, etc.) already used this app's own dark theme. Sitting inside an
otherwise consistently dark UI, that light-grey rectangle read as a foreign
window.

Fix: the outer panel background, axis tick labels, axis titles, and the
density-key border now use the app's own theme colours (`PANEL`, `MUTED`,
`TEXT`, `GRID`). The parts that are deliberately LabVIEW-accurate are
unchanged: the plotting rectangle itself is still black, the universal
semicircle is still red, and the violet→blue→cyan→green→yellow→red
density ramp and its colour key are untouched.

## Added: chi-square (goodness of fit) display

- A new **MEAN χ² (valid px)** tile in the top metrics row, alongside valid
  pixels / mean τ / representative τ / max counts.
- A new **Chi-square** option in the Overview's "SECOND MAP" dropdown,
  rendered with a cool-to-hot ("turbo") palette so a good fit (χ² ≈ 1)
  reads as blue/violet and a poor fit reads as red. Its subtitle reports
  mean/median χ² and valid-pixel count, and follows ROI selection the same
  way the phasor and histogram panels already do (whole image vs. selected
  ROI).
- `chi_square_image()` / `chi_square_stats()` added to `rendering.py` for
  reuse (e.g. by future exports).

This is also the natural place to actually see the effect of the "Noise
model" setting discussed previously: differences between noise-weighting
choices often show up more clearly in χ² than in the fitted τ itself,
especially in dim/noisy regions.

## Verification

- New checks in `tests/gui_smoke_v10.py`: mean χ² populates after analysis,
  "Chi-square" appears in the secondary-map choices, its subtitle correctly
  reports "whole image" vs. "selected ROI" scope, and a corner pixel of a
  freshly rendered phasor plot exactly matches the app's panel background
  colour (guards against the LabVIEW-grey regression coming back).
