# FLIM Studio 0.10.0

## Lifetime display scaling

- Added **Auto / Manual** lifetime scaling in the Lifetime presentation controls.
- Manual mode uses the entered minimum and maximum lifetime.
- Auto mode calculates robust limits from the finite lifetime-map 1st and 99th percentiles.
- Scaling updates the lifetime image, HSV composite, lifetime histogram, and native component maps immediately without rerunning the analysis.
- Auto-mode outliers saturate at the LUT endpoints rather than being marked invalid. Numerical lifetime values and fit results are never changed by display scaling.

## Multi-exponential results

- Added a dedicated **Multi-exponential** tab.
- Native bi- and tri-exponential analyses show paired lifetime and local-amplitude maps: tau1/A1, tau2/A2, and tau3/A3.
- Components retain the native presentation order, longest lifetime to shortest lifetime.
- The principal Lifetime map remains the amplitude-weighted mean lifetime.
- Global-fit shared lifetimes and successful pixel-fit counts are stated in the tab summary when available.
