# FLIM Studio 0.11.6

## Phasor and Histogram are separate tabs again

Reverts the v0.11.4 tab merge: **Phasor** and **Histogram** are back to
being their own full-width tabs, as they were originally. The
`phasor_plot()` square-plot fix (v0.11.3) is unaffected — the plot itself
stays a proper square with matching G/S scale, just displayed at whatever
size its own tab gives it, same as any other full-tab view.

The Overview tab's compact "both together" panel from v0.11.5 (the
"Selected phasors" area next to "Selected-point decays") is untouched —
that was the change you actually wanted; this only reverts the dedicated
Phasor/Histogram tabs back to being separate.

## Verification

- `tests/gui_smoke_v10.py`'s layout check updated to confirm Phasor and
  Histogram now live in *different* parent frames again (each in its own
  tab's column 0), rather than sharing one.
